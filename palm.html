OIL

We aim to avoid palm oil

Why palm oil needs consideration

  • Palm oil contains a substantial amount of saturated fat, including palmitic acid, which can raise LDL cholesterol when it replaces unsaturated fats.
  • Refining at high temperatures can form contaminants such as 3-MCPD esters and glycidyl esters.
  • It is widely used because it is relatively inexpensive and solid at room temperature.
  • Repeated heating can increase fat oxidation.

What we may use instead

Cold-pressed or expeller-pressed unsaturated oils such as rice bran oil, high-oleic sunflower oil and groundnut oil. The choice must consider fatty-acid profile, processing, stability, cost and food safety.

Illustrative yearly intake calculation

Assumption: about 3 g palm oil per biscuit. Two to three biscuits daily would provide 6–9 g oil per day, or approximately 2.2–3.3 kg per year.
