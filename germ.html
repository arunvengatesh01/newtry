GERM IN WHEAT

1. What it contains

  • Vitamin E (tocopherols), thiamine (B1) and folate
  • Healthy unsaturated fats (linoleic acid)
  • High-quality protein and glutathione
  • Spermidine, a polyamine linked to cell renewal

2. Why it is important for health

  • Antioxidant protection for cells and blood vessels
  • Folate for cell division and a healthy nervous system
  • Healthy fats support heart and brain function

3. Without it: possible effects

  • Lower vitamin E, folate and B1 intake
  • Less natural protection against oxidative stress
  • Biscuits may be nutritionally less varied even if they look wheaty

4. Rare and professional insights

  • The germ is only about 2.5% of the kernel, but it is the living embryo of the grain.
  • Industry removes it because its oil can go rancid, shortening shelf life.
  • Wheat germ is among the richer food sources of spermidine.
  • Wheat germ oil contains octacosanol, which has been studied for endurance.
