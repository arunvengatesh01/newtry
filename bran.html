BRAN IN WHEAT

1. What it contains

  • Dietary fibre (mostly insoluble arabinoxylan, roughly 40% of bran)
  • B vitamins: niacin, B6 and thiamine
  • Iron, zinc, magnesium, phosphorus and manganese
  • Antioxidant phenolics such as ferulic acid and lignans

2. Why it is important for health

  • Keeps digestion regular and feeds beneficial gut bacteria
  • Slows glucose absorption and helps you feel full longer
  • Supports healthy cholesterol and lowers colon stress

3. Without it: possible effects

  • Constipation, bloating and sluggish digestion
  • Faster blood sugar spikes and early hunger
  • Lower mineral and B-vitamin intake

4. Rare and professional insights

  • Bran is only about 14% of the grain yet holds most of its fibre and minerals.
  • Most ferulic acid is bound to fibre and is released by gut bacteria.
  • The aleurone layer, rich in B vitamins, is milled away with bran during refining.
  • Baking and fermentation can reduce phytic acid, improving mineral absorption.
