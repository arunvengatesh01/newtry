Bran: the fibre layer

  • The outer coat of the grain and its main source of dietary fibre.
  • Fibre supports digestion and helps you feel full for longer.
  • Bran also carries B vitamins and minerals such as iron and magnesium.
  • Refined white flour (maida) is milled to remove most of it.
